Industrial IoT · Solar

Solar Site Monitoring Solution

Monitoring across 1,500 utility and commercial solar sites, processing 200,000 data points per second for real-time anomaly detection and alerting. Reporting runs through Kepware and OSI PI connectors, while free-text search and MAXIMO-enabled workflows streamline service requests and alarm management.

  • Reduced asset downtime: continuous collection, analysis, and alerting before service disruption.
  • Higher power productivity: less downtime translated straight into production gains.
  • Lower legal & financial liability: fleet-level visibility catches hazards before penalties.
  • Condition-based maintenance: historical and condition data replaced fixed schedules.

Utility Field Service & Maintenance Management

Network Security

Global Network Monitoring for a VWAN Provider

A globally deployed monitoring solution that continuously watches the attack surface, network paths, exposed ports, and services. Real-time visibility and alerting enable swift response to potential threats, cutting the risk of outside-in attacks on critical infrastructure.

  • Unified inventory & asset management: identify, access, prioritize, and remediate across all connected assets, agentlessly.
  • Accelerated visibility: precise, rapid insight from real-time monitoring.
  • Comprehensive risk management: operational and security risk overseen in one place.

Confidential Compute · Oil & Gas

Confidential Edge Computing for an Oil & Gas Provider

End-to-end porting of applications, services, and compute onto confidential-compute hardware at the edge. Kubernetes orchestration and a confidential-compute service mesh underpin it; the CI/CD pipeline configures and tests inside the secure enclave, with encryption protecting code, data, and configuration.

  • Trustless security for key IP: AI/ML models and software modules stay protected even on customer-provided hardware.
  • Regular upgrades: CI/CD keeps components current inside the confidential environment.
  • Performance monitoring: a built-in observability stack surfaces key metrics.
